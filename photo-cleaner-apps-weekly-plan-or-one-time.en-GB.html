Photo cleaner apps: weekly plan or one-time unlock?

When your iPhone says storage is full, a photo cleaner app looks like a quick fix. Most are free to download, but many charge by the week once you want to delete in bulk, and a weekly plan left running adds up quickly. Here is how four popular cleaners charge on the UK App Store, and when our one-time-unlock app PicClear Pro is, and is not, the better fit.

PicClear Pro is our app. We describe the other apps using only what their public App Store pages say, and we list our own weak spots too. Checked on 27 November 2026 on the UK App Store. Prices, offers and ratings change; check the current listing before you buy. App Store pages show up to 10 purchase items, so a list may not be complete.

Over a year

  • Cleanup, Swipewipe and the Clever Cleaner weekly plan: a weekly plan renews every week until you cancel it, so it can be charged 52 times in a year.
  • Cleaner Kit: its description says full access is billed weekly or annually, and renews until you turn it off.
  • Clever Cleaner “Lifetime” and PicClear Pro “Full Unlock”: you pay once.

A photo clean-up is usually a once-in-a-while job. On the UK App Store, many of the weekly items listed above cost more for a single week than PicClear Pro’s one-time unlock, and Clever Cleaner’s “Lifetime” costs more than it too. If you only need one big clear-out and you cancel straight away, one week of a plan may be all you pay. The risk is forgetting to cancel.

Where PicClear Pro is a good fit

  • You pay once. Scan and preview for free; when you’re ready, one Full Unlock on the App Store enables Smart Cleanup. There’s no subscription to cancel later.
  • It finds the usual space-wasters: duplicates, similar shots and bursts (grouped with a suggested best photo to keep), screenshots, blurry shots and space-hungry videos.
  • You review before anything goes. You preview everything before anything is deleted, and Favourites are never selected automatically. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos, so you can still recover them for a while.
  • Private. Everything is analysed on your iPhone. No account, no ads and no cloud uploads, and it works fully offline.

Where PicClear Pro is not the right choice

  • Cleaning in bulk needs the unlock. On iPhone, clearing whole groups in one go needs the Full Unlock. On Android, the Google Play listing says deleting needs an in-app purchase. If you only want a small tidy-up, Clever Cleaner describes free daily limits that may cost you nothing.
  • Photos and videos only. PicClear doesn’t clean contacts, email or other storage. Cleanup and Cleaner Kit, for example, list contact and email clean-up as well.
  • The space comes back later. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted first, so you get the space back once that is emptied.
  • It’s new. PicClear Pro has no UK App Store ratings yet, so you have less to go on than with the apps above.
  • Separate stores, separate purchases. Buying on the App Store doesn’t unlock the Android app, and the Google Play price may differ.

A quick way to check any cleaner app

  1. Before you scan, open the app’s App Store page and scroll to In-App Purchases. “Week” or “weekly” in a name usually means a plan that renews every week.
  2. Check whether the free version lets you delete anything, or only shows you what it found.
  3. If you take a weekly plan for one clear-out, set a reminder to cancel it in your Apple ID’s Subscriptions settings.
  4. Read a few of the newest 1–3 star reviews, not just the average rating.
