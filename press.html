PicClear Pro: Photo Cleaner
Press & media

PicClear Pro is a private photo cleaner: it finds duplicates, similar shots, old screenshots, blurry photos and large videos on your device, and deletes nothing until you confirm.

重複写真、類似写真、古いスクリーンショット、ぼやけた写真、大きな動画を端末上で見つけ、確認するまで何も削除しない写真整理アプリ。

Key features

  • Finds duplicate photos, similar photos and bursts, screenshots, blurry photos, large videos and large photos
  • Review every group with thumbnail previews before anything is deleted
  • Smart selection keeps a suggested photo in each group; Favorites are never selected automatically
  • Removed items go to the system's recently deleted area or trash first and can be restored for a limited time (on Android, on newer system versions)
  • Everything is analyzed on the device; photos are never uploaded; works fully offline
  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics
  • Scan and preview for free; an in-app purchase unlocks cleanup
