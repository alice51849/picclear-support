A photo cleaner that works offline: clean up your iPhone photos without uploading them

Many photo cleaners want an account, a connection or a cloud upload before they show you anything. If you are on a patchy mobile signal, watching your data, or simply do not want your family photos leaving the phone, that is the wrong trade. PicClear Pro analyses your library on your iPhone, needs no account and works fully offline. Here is what that means in practice and how to free up space with it.

A quick way to check any cleaner yourself: switch on Aeroplane Mode and open it. If it still scans and shows results, the work is happening on your phone.

What it finds without a connection

  • Duplicates: identical copies taking up double the space.
  • Similar shots and bursts: grouped together, with a suggested best photo to keep.
  • Screenshots: old ones you no longer need.
  • Blurry shots: photos you would never share.
  • Space-hungry videos: usually the biggest single gain.

Clean up in four steps

  1. Check where your space went. Open Settings → General → iPhone Storage and note the size shown next to Photos, so you can compare afterwards.
  2. Scan. Open PicClear Pro and let it scan your library on the device.
  3. Preview. Go through each group. You preview everything before anything is deleted, and Favourites are never auto-selected.
  4. Clear and compare. Remove what you selected (one-tap Smart Cleanup comes with the one-time unlock; scanning and previewing are free), then look at iPhone Storage again to see how much space you got back.

Safety net before and after you delete

  • Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos. Apple says you can recover them from there for 30 days.
  • If iCloud Photos is on, Apple notes that deleting a photo on one device also deletes it on your other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Back up anything you want to keep first.

Frequently asked questions

Does PicClear Pro need an internet connection?

No. It works fully offline. Your library is analysed on your iPhone, so you can scan and review on a flight, on a weak mobile signal or with data switched off.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. There are no cloud uploads and no account. Everything is analysed on your iPhone.

Will it delete photos on its own?

No. You preview everything before anything is deleted, and Favourites are never auto-selected. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in Photos.

Is it a subscription?

No. Scanning and previewing are free. A one-time unlock enables Smart Cleanup, which clears whole groups of duplicates and similar shots in one go. No subscription and no ads.

I removed the wrong photo. Can I get it back?

Usually, yes: open the Recently Deleted album in Photos. Apple says deleted photos and videos can be recovered there for 30 days.
