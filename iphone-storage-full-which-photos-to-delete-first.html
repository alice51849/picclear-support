iPhone storage full? Which photos and videos to delete first

When the “Storage Almost Full” banner shows up, most people start deleting photos one by one from the top of the library. That is the slowest way to win space back. A handful of large videos and a pile of duplicates usually free more room than an evening of scrolling. Below is the order that works, three checks to do before you delete anything, and how to do the whole job on your iPhone without uploading a single photo.

Step 0: confirm that Photos is really the problem

Open Settings → General → iPhone Storage. Apple shows a bar of what is using the space, recommendations, and a list of apps sorted by size. If Photos sits at the top of the list, cleaning the library is the right move. If an app or its downloaded files sit at the top instead, offloading or deleting that app frees space faster than any photo cleanup.

Write down the number next to Photos before you start. It is the only honest way to see whether the cleanup worked.

Delete in this order: biggest gain first, least regret first

OrderWhat to look forWhy it comes here
1Large videosOne long 4K clip can weigh as much as hundreds of photos. A few deletions move the storage bar more than anything else.
2Exact duplicatesIdentical copies take double the space and you lose nothing when one copy goes.
3Bursts and near-identical shotsTen frames of the same moment: keep one or two, remove the rest.
4Old screenshotsBoarding passes, delivery codes and chats you already acted on. Rarely worth keeping.
5Blurry shotsOut-of-focus photos you would never share.
6Large photosHigh-resolution images you can thin out last, once the easy wins are done.

The rule behind the order: start where the gain per decision is highest and the risk of regret is lowest. Duplicates are zero-regret; a blurry photo of a grandparent might not be, so it comes later and deserves a second look.

Three checks before you delete anything

  1. Know whether iCloud Photos is on. Apple notes that when you use iCloud Photos and delete a photo or video on one device, it is also deleted on all other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Cleaning the iPhone then cleans the iPad and the Mac too.
  2. Back up anything you might want later. Apple recommends backing up the photos and videos you still want before deleting.
  3. Remember the safety net. Deleted photos and videos go to the Recently Deleted album, and Apple says you can recover them from there for 30 days.
